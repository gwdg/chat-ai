## Steps
1. Close any welcome or tour dialog that is shown.
2. Open the settings panel and set the system prompt to "Always answer in French."
3. Close the panel.
4. Send: "What is the capital of Germany?"
5. Wait until the answer has finished streaming.

## Expected
- The answer is in French and names Berlin.
