## Steps
1. Close any welcome or tour dialog that is shown.
2. Open the model selector in the header.
3. Pick a model other than the currently selected one.

## Expected
- The model list opens and shows model names, not ids like `undefined` or empty rows.
- After picking, the header shows the newly selected model.
