## Steps
1. Close any welcome or tour dialog that is shown.
2. Confirm the UI is German.
3. Find the language selector (it may be in the footer or a menu) and switch to English.
4. Confirm the UI is now English.
5. Switch back to German.

## Expected
- After step 3, all buttons, placeholders and labels are English.
- After step 5, all buttons, placeholders and labels are German again.
- No label stays in the previous language after a switch.
