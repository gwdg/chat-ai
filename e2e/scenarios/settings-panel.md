## Steps
1. Close any welcome or tour dialog that is shown.
2. Open the settings panel (gear icon).
3. Look at the available options, then close the panel.

## Expected
- The panel shows labeled controls, e.g. system prompt, temperature and top_p.
- Help icons and labels are translated text, not message keys.
- The panel closes and the chat is usable again.
