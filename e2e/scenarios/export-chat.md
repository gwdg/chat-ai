## Steps
1. Close any welcome or tour dialog that is shown.
2. Send: "Say hi."
3. Wait until the answer has finished streaming.
4. Open the export option of the conversation.

## Expected
- An export dialog or menu offers at least one format (e.g. JSON, PDF, text).
- Choosing a format closes the dialog without an error.
