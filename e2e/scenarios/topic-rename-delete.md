## Steps
1. Close any welcome or tour dialog that is shown.
2. Create a topic named "Old Name".
3. Rename it to "New Name".
4. Delete it and confirm if asked.

## Expected
- After step 3 the sidebar shows "New Name" and not "Old Name".
- After step 4 the topic is gone from the sidebar.
