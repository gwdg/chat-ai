## Steps
1. Close any welcome or tour dialog that is shown.
2. Open the sidebar via the menu button.
3. Close it again.

## Expected
- The sidebar covers the chat as a drawer and lists conversations or an empty-state message.
- After closing, the prompt box is fully visible and usable.
- No horizontal scrolling at any time.
