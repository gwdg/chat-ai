## Steps
1. Close any welcome or tour dialog that is shown.
2. Type into the prompt box: "Explain in two sentences what a large language model is."
3. Send the message.
4. Wait until the answer has finished streaming.

## Expected
- The question appears in the conversation as a user message.
- The assistant's answer is non-empty, readable and relates to the question.
- The new conversation appears in the sidebar (on mobile, open the sidebar to check).
- No error message is shown.
