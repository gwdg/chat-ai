*This page describes GAIA as it is offered in this app. GWDG operates the service and its models on behalf of the University of Göttingen.*

GAIA is the AI study assistant for students of the University of Göttingen. You can ask it questions, work through material with it, and have it help you write and revise texts. Please read this page before you start so you know what to expect — and what not to expect.

## What you can do with GAIA

- Ask a question about your subject and have the answer explained in different words, with an example, or step by step.
- Upload a script, a set of slides or a paper and ask for a summary, an explanation or a list of the key terms.
- Draft, shorten, translate and revise texts.
- Have your own reasoning checked, or ask for practice questions on a topic.
- Open a course page (for example C programming) and work through its skills, a practice quiz and self-learning pages.

Everything happens in a normal conversation. Your chats are stored in this browser, not in a course account.

## What GAIA is not

This app is a general chat assistant, not a course platform. In particular it has

- no graded exercises: the practice quiz on a course page is graded locally, stores no progress and counts towards no grade,
- no tutoring sessions, learning plans or progress tracking,
- no automatic assessment of what you have understood,
- no search over the course material of a lecture,
- no connection to Stud.IP, examination offices or course administration.

Your lecturer's slides and exercise sheets are not automatically available to GAIA. If you want it to work with a document, you have to upload that document yourself.

## How reliable are the answers?

GAIA is built on large language models. These models produce fluent text that can be wrong: invented facts, invented sources, wrong calculations and outdated information are all possible. Treat every answer as a draft that you check against your course material.

Do not paste other people's personal data into a chat, and do not use GAIA to make decisions that require a qualified person.

## Where your data goes

Chats, uploaded files and settings stay in your browser's local storage on this device. When you send a message, its content is transmitted to the language model selected in the chat header and processed there. Which model that is, and what that means for your data, is described on the [data protection page](https://gaia.uni-goettingen.de/legal/data-protection).

You can review and change what you have agreed to in the profile under *Chat preferences → Your consents*.
