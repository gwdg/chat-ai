*This statement describes the accessibility of GAIA in this app. It is maintained together with the authoritative statement at [gaia.uni-goettingen.de/accessibility](https://gaia.uni-goettingen.de/accessibility).*

GAIA should be usable by all students, including those who navigate with a keyboard, a screen reader, a braille display or magnification. This page summarises what the app provides, which limitations we know of, and where to report a barrier.

## What the app provides

- Every control can be reached and operated with the keyboard alone, and the focused control is visible.
- Buttons that only show an icon carry an accessible name, and information is not conveyed by colour alone.
- The interface follows the browser's text size and zoom level.
- There is a light and a dark appearance, plus additional colour themes; the choice is remembered.
- The document language is declared, and English and German can be switched in the footer. A chosen language is remembered.
- No audio or video starts automatically, and no animation is required to understand a page.
- Pages have one main heading, and lists and sections are marked up as such.

## Known limitations

- **Answers are model output.** Their content, structure and length cannot be guaranteed. They can be wrong or hard to read, and this applies to screen-reader users as well.
- **Diagrams and formulas.** Answers may contain Mermaid, Typst, LaTeX or TikZ blocks. They are rendered as graphics (SVG), which a screen reader can only announce in a limited way. The source stays available next to the graphic and can be read there.
- **Uploaded documents.** A scanned PDF without a text layer cannot be read out; the extraction depends on the file.
- **Exported PDFs** are not tagged PDF/UA.
- **Models and browser chrome** are outside the app's control: the answer quality and the behaviour of the browser's own dialogs depend on the provider and the browser.

## Report a barrier

If something stops you, please tell us. Use the bug report button in the footer and choose the type *Accessibility*, or write to **gaia-barrierefreiheit@uni-goettingen.de**. Describe what you were doing, which assistive technology you use and what happened. We will look into it and answer.

Your feedback also helps us improve this statement: it is reviewed regularly and updated when the app changes.

## Enforcement

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the arbitration office under the Lower Saxony Equal Opportunities for People with Disabilities Act:

Schlichtungsstelle nach dem Niedersächsischen Behindertengleichstellungsgesetz
Niedersächsisches Ministerium für Soziales, Arbeit, Gesundheit und Gleichstellung
E-Mail: schlichtungsstelle@ms.niedersachsen.de
